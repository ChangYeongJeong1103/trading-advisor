# Moments When Trump Truth Social Posts Moved the Stock Market: A Comprehensive Timeline (January 20, 2025 – May 15, 2026)

## Executive Summary

During the first 16 months of the second Trump administration, **Truth Social effectively became the single most powerful market-moving instrument**. One post written directly by Trump drove the S&P 500 up +9.52% (April 9, 2025 "BUY" + 90-day pause), while another sent the NASDAQ down -3.56% (October 10, 2025 China rare-earth retaliation threat).

This report focuses strictly on **market reactions where causality is clearly documented to a Truth Social post** — excluding White House press conferences, Oval Office remarks, Air Force One comments, signing-ceremony remarks, and interviews — and is organized chronologically by phase. It includes sector-specific analysis tailored to the user's profile as a display engineer with exposure to Korean memory semiconductors (Samsung, SK Hynix) and US semis (NVDA, AVGO, AMD, etc.).

**Key Patterns (recurring)**:
- **High posting frequency just before/after the market open** — 9:30–9:40 AM ET (April 9 "BUY"), or pre-market (May 23 EU/Apple)
- **Friday after-close or weekend posting = "news dump" pattern** (especially Iran military action, Colombia tariffs)
- The semiconductor sector reacts to China-related posts with **2–3x beta** versus the S&P
- Trump himself has acknowledged market influence — Lutnick: *"Bessent and I sat with the President while he wrote one of the most extraordinary Truth posts of his Presidency"* (April 9)
- **Formation of the "TACO trade" pattern** (Trump Always Chickens Out) — from H2 2025 the market progressively discounted extreme threats

### January 26, 2025 (Sunday) — Colombia tariff threat
**Post content**: Trump posted on Truth Social that Colombia had refused US military deportation flights and announced "emergency 25% tariffs on all goods... rising to 50% in one week," visa sanctions, and financial penalties. *"These measures are just the beginning."*

**Market reaction**: As a weekend post, immediate intraday reaction was limited. President Petro capitulated that same night, so risk-off cleared quickly. On Monday 1/27 the S&P 500 was nearly flat (~-0.3%). **Implication**: The start of a pattern of using Truth Social as a policy-announcement channel — this became the template for all subsequent tariff announcements.

### February 1, 2025 (Saturday) — Mexico/Canada 25% + China 10% tariffs take effect
**Post content**: Trump invoked IEEPA and confirmed on Truth Social 25% tariffs on Mexico/Canada and 10% on China. Fentanyl inflow was cited as justification.

**Market reaction (Monday 2/3)**: Strong risk-off. The S&P 500 dropped close to -2% intraday and closed -0.8% (recovered after Mexico delay announcement). NASDAQ -1.2%. Semiconductors weak — NVDA -2.8%, AVGO -2.4%.

### February 13, 2025 — Hints at reciprocal-tariff memo
**Post content**: Trump announced reciprocal tariff plans and directed staff to investigate trade barriers. First mention of *"April 2 Liberation Day"* in a Truth Social post.
**Market reaction**: Immediate impact small, but a medium-term overhang formed — this set the stage for the major selloff in early April.

### February 27, 2025 (Thursday morning) — March 4 tariffs confirmed
**Post content**: *"We cannot allow this scourge to continue to harm the USA, and therefore, until it stops, or is seriously limited, the proposed TARIFFS scheduled to go into effect on MARCH FOURTH will, indeed, go into effect, as scheduled."* — an additional 10% on China was applied the same day.

**Market reaction**: Sharp drop in S&P 500 futures right after the announcement. Feb 27 close: -1.6%, NASDAQ -2.8%, **NVDA singly -8.5%** (NVIDIA earnings + tariff fears combo).

### March 3, 2025 (Monday afternoon) — March 4 effective date confirmed
**Market reaction**: Dow -1.76% (-800 pts), S&P -2.64% (worst of the year), NASDAQ -3.0%, **NVDA -9%** (tariffs + China-export concerns combined). **This day is classified as a Truth Social post + Oval Office remarks combo catalyst.**

## Phase 2: Liberation Day and Its Aftermath (April 2 – April 9, 2025)

These seven days were **the most volatile week for the stock market in the post-COVID era** — Deutsche Bank described it as an *"absolutely seismic month."*

### April 2, 2025 (Wednesday, ~3:50 PM ET announcement) — "LIBERATION DAY IN AMERICA!"
**Post content**: Trump posted *"LIBERATION DAY IN AMERICA!"* on Truth Social, then unveiled the reciprocal-tariff chart in the White House Rose Garden. Country-by-country tariff rates were shared via Truth Social — China 34%, EU 20%, Vietnam 46%, Taiwan 32%, Japan 24%, **Korea 25%**, India 26%, Cambodia 49%.

**Market reaction (after-close + April 3)**:
- After-hours (4/2): SPY -2.2%, QQQ -3.0%, Apple -7%, Nike -7%, NVDA -4.5%, Tesla -6%
- Regular session April 3: **S&P 500 -4.84%**, **NASDAQ -5.97%**, **SOX -9.9%** (worst single-day for semis)
- By name: **TSM -7.2%, AVGO -10.5%, NVDA -7.8%, AMD -8.9%, MU -16%** (Vietnam/Taiwan/Korea all high-tariff targets)

### April 3–4, 2025 — Consecutive selloff + China retaliatory tariff (34%)
- April 4: S&P **-5.97%**, NASDAQ -5.82%. **2-day decline was the 5th worst since WWII.** Over 7 days the S&P 500 fell more than 12%.
- Trump Truth Social: *"MY POLICIES WILL NEVER CHANGE!"*, *"This is a great time to get rich!"* — exhorting people to buy (a prelude to the April 9 "BUY" post).

Date	Trump-related event	
2025-04-03	First trading day after the “Liberation Day” reciprocal-tariff announcement. The chip sector was hit much harder than the broad market — due to supply-chain exposure of Apple/Nvidia/TSMC, etc.
2025-04-04	China announced 34% retaliatory tariffs. Two consecutive tariff-driven crashes. The S&P 500 fell close to 10% in two days; the Nasdaq confirmed bear-market entry.

### April 7, 2025 (Monday) — Fake "90-day pause" rumor
**Market reaction**: At one point a fake *"Hassett confirms 90-day pause"* headline circulated, briefly sending the S&P 500 up +8% intraday, before Trump denied it on Truth Social and it crashed again — a **"fake news rally"** in which ~$2.4 trillion of market cap swung. Trump's own Truth Social denial was the catalyst.

### **★ April 9, 2025 (Wednesday) — Historic Turning Point ★**

**(1) 9:33 AM ET post**: *"BE COOL! Everything is going to work out well. The USA will be bigger and better than ever before!"*

**(2) 9:37 AM ET post**: **"THIS IS A GREAT TIME TO BUY!!! DJT"** ← the most famous post

**(3) ~1:18 PM ET post**: Trump announced a 90-day pause on reciprocal tariffs on Truth Social (10% baseline maintained, **China raised to 145%**).

**Market reaction**:
- **S&P 500: +9.52%** — largest single-day gain since October 2008, $4 trillion in market cap recovered (70% of the prior 4-day losses)
- **NASDAQ Composite: +12.16%** — **largest single-day gain since January 2001**
- **Dow: +7.87% (+2,962 pts)**
- **Semiconductors explode higher**: **SOX +18.8%** — largest single-day gain in the index's history. **NVDA +18.7%, AMD +24%, AVGO +15%, TSM +14%, MU +18%, INTC +13%**
- **DJT (Trump Media): +22.7%** — double the broader market (the "DJT" sign-off in the 9:37 post had a dual interpretation — the president's initials OR the Trump Media ticker)

Date	Trump-related event
2025-04-09	Truth Social "THIS IS A GREAT TIME TO BUY!!!" followed by a 90-day tariff pause announcement. The strongest bullish reversal on the Trump timeline. Tariff-panic short-covering plus a tech relief rally fired simultaneously.

**Insider-trading controversy (a key user concern)**:
- 9:37 AM "BUY" post → 90-day pause announced ~4 hours later → buyers booked large profits
- AOC: *"Any member of Congress who purchased stocks in the last 48 hours should probably disclose that now. I've been hearing some interesting chatter on the floor."*
- Sen. Adam Schiff: *"These constant gyrations in policy provide dangerous opportunities for insider trading."* — formal investigation request
- Lutnick (CNBC): *"Bessent and I sat with the President while he wrote one of the most extraordinary Truth posts of his Presidency"* — a **strong implication that the BUY post was written when the pause decision had already been made**
- Richard Painter (former White House ethics counsel): *"The people who bought when they saw that post made a lot of money."*
- Trump follow-up after midnight (12:45 AM ET 4/10): *"What a day, but more great days coming!!!"*
- **Why this matters for a display/semiconductor engineer**: Buying SOXL or NVDA calls concurrently with the 9:37 AM post could have yielded +30–50% intraday. No formal SEC enforcement action followed, but the matter remains on the congressional-hearing agenda.

## Phase 3: Tariff-Reprieve Rally & Middle-East Trip Period (April 10 – Late May 2025)

### April 10, 2025 (Thursday) — 145% China tariff confirmed
**Post content**: Trump clarified on Truth Social that the China cumulative tariff was 145% ("125% reciprocal + 20% fentanyl").
**Market reaction**: Hangover from the prior day's surge. S&P **-3.46%**, NASDAQ -4.31%, **SOX -8%** (NVDA -6%, AMD -7%, MU -6%) — chip names with the largest China exposure gave back the most.

### April 15–16, 2025 — NVIDIA H20 export-license requirement (market impact)
**Context**: The Commerce Department notified NVIDIA that H20 chip exports required a license. Trump did not announce directly on Truth Social — this was issued as administration policy. **NVDA: -6.9%**, NVDA expected ~$2.5B Q1 revenue loss. SOX -3.1%, AMD (MI308 concerns) -4.3%.

### April 17 & 21, 2025 — "Too Late" attacks on Powell
**April 17 post**: *"Powell's termination cannot come fast enough."*

**April 21 (Easter Monday) post**: *"There can almost be no inflation, but there can be a SLOWING of the economy unless Mr Too Late, a major loser, lowers interest rates, NOW."* + ECB comparison, *"Powell has always been 'Too Late.'"*

**Market reaction (4/21)**:
- **S&P 500: -2.36%** — Powell-firing risk + Fed-independence concerns
- **NASDAQ: -2.55%**
- **USD index -1.0%, gold +2.9%** (safe-asset avoidance pattern)
- Semis: SOX -2.8%, NVDA -4.5%

### May 8, 2025 — UK trade deal announced
**Post content**: Trump announced on Truth Social an *"Economic Prosperity Deal"* with the UK — the first major trade deal under the reciprocal framework.
**Market reaction**: S&P +0.6%, NASDAQ +1.1%. Market read as *"first deal proves the framework works."*

### May 11–12, 2025 — Geneva US-China Tariff Détente
**Post content**: Trump posted on Truth Social Sunday night: *"TOTAL RESET... BIG PROGRESS!!!"* On Monday May 12 he confirmed a reduction of China tariffs from 145% → 30% (for 90 days). EO 14298 took effect.

**Market reaction (Mon 5/12)**:
- **S&P 500: +3.26%** — second-largest single-day gain of the year
- **NASDAQ: +4.35%**
- **SOX: +7.0%** — NVDA +5.4%, AMD +5.2%, AVGO +6.3%, TSM +4.4%
- May 13: S&P 500 turned positive year-to-date for the first time

### May 12–16, 2025 — "Buy Stocks" messaging ahead of Middle-East trip (user memory)
**Pattern**: April 9 "BUY" post → 90-day pause → May 12 "RESET" post → China deal → May 13–16 Saudi (13) / Qatar (14) / UAE (15) trip. The **consecutive timing** revived insider-trading concerns. Trump also posted many market-optimistic *"Tremendous deals being made"*-style posts during the trip.

### May 23, 2025 (Friday morning, pre-market) — Apple 25% + EU 50% twin posts
**Truth Social twin posts (30 min apart)**:
1. **Apple post** (8:47 AM ET): *"I have long ago informed Tim Cook of Apple that I expect their iPhone's that will be sold in the United States of America will be manufactured and built in the United States, not India, or anyplace else. If that is not the case, a Tariff of at least 25% must be paid by Apple to the U.S."*

2. **EU post** (9:23 AM ET): *"The European Union, which was formed for the primary purpose of taking advantage of the United States on TRADE... Our discussions with them are going nowhere! Therefore, I am recommending a straight 50% Tariff on the European Union, starting on June 1, 2025."*

**Market reaction**:
- Dow futures -600 pts pre-market
- **S&P 500: -0.67%** (close), weekly -1.7% (worst since the first week of May)
- **NASDAQ: -1.00%**
- **AAPL: -3.0%** (single name dragged the Dow)
- Semis (EU exposure): ASML -2.5%, NXPI -2.2%, NVDA -1.2%
- **CBOE VIX**: spiked +23% intraday before closing +8%
- Europe: STOXX 600 -1.7%, DAX -2.4%, CAC -2.2%

### May 25, 2025 (Sunday) — EU extension to July 9 (reversal of the twin posts)
**Post**: Trump on Truth Social: *"I have agreed to the extension — July 9, 2025"* (after a call with Ursula von der Leyen).
**Market reaction (Mon 5/27)**: S&P +2.05%, NASDAQ +2.5%, SOX +3.5% — **a 50% threat fully reversed in just 2 days, marking the start of the "TACO trade" (Trump Always Chickens Out) meme.**

## Phase 4: Iran/Israel 12-Day War (June 2025)

### June 4, 2025 — Steel/Aluminum 25% → 50% increase (confirmed on Truth Social)
**Post content**: *"It is my great honor to raise the Tariffs on steel and aluminum from 25% to 50%, effective Wednesday, June 4th. Our steel and aluminum industries are coming back like never before... MAKE AMERICA GREAT AGAIN!"*
**Market reaction**: S&P -0.6%, NASDAQ -0.5%. US steel stocks (CLF, X, STLD, NUE) surged +20–25%. Appliance/auto stocks (Whirlpool, GM, F) weak.

### June 13, 2025 — Israel's first strike on Iran's nuclear facilities
**Truth Social context**: That morning Trump posted in the form *"Israel acted alone... but we knew,"* hinting at US involvement.
**Market reaction**: WTI +7%, S&P -1.1%, NASDAQ -1.3%. Airlines (DAL, UAL) -4–5%. Energy (XOM, CVX) +2%. Defense (LMT, RTX, NOC) +3%.

**Market reaction (Mon 6/23 open)**:
- Initial gap-down: S&P futures -1.2%, Brent +5.7%
- Close: S&P -0.4%, NASDAQ -0.7% (weaker than expected — risk-off subsided once Hormuz wasn't closed)
- Defense (LMT, RTX, NOC) +1–2%, energy +2%
- Semis little changed (-0.5%)
- **Pattern**: Saturday-evening announcement → "news dump" effect kept Monday's gap limited

## Phase 5: Trade Agreements & Court Rulings (July – December 2025)

### May 28, 2025 — US Court of International Trade rules IEEPA tariffs illegal
**Truth Social reaction**: Trump posted heated messages such as *"HORRIBLE COUNTRY-DESTROYING DECISION."* (Actual market impact was minimal because the appeals court immediately issued a stay.)

**Truth Social post**: *"I have just made a Trade Deal with Vietnam"* — 20% tariff (reduced from 46%). Southeast Asian semiconductor supply-chain relief.
**Market reaction**: S&P +0.5%, SOX +1.2%. NXPI, MCHP +2% (Vietnam packaging exposure).

### July 7–8, 2025 — Korea 25% "Tariff Letter"
**Post**: Trump posted directly on Truth Social a *"tariff letter"* addressed to the Korean president. 25% threat ("Dear Mr. President").
**Market reaction**: KOSPI -1.5%, SK Hynix ADR -3%, Samsung ADR -2.2%. Minimal impact on US markets.

### July 11, 2025 — Canada 35% tariff letter
**Post**: Trump on Truth Social threatened a 35% Canada tariff effective August 1 ("Dear Mr. Prime Minister Mark Carney"). Cited fentanyl inflow as justification.
**Market reaction**: S&P -0.33%, NASDAQ -0.22% — market had learned the "TACO trade" pattern. Canadian dollar -0.8%.

### July 15, 2025 — Hints at restarting NVIDIA H20 exports to China
**Post**: Trump suggested on Truth Social: *"We are letting Nvidia sell its H20 chip to China."* Commerce Department license approved.
**Market reaction**: **NVDA +4.0%**, **AMD +6.4%**, SOX +1.7%. S&P +0.4%. **Sole catalyst for the semiconductor sector.**
- August 11 follow-up: Deal requiring **15% of H20 revenue paid to the US government**. NVDA briefly -1% before recovering.

### July 23, 2025 — Japan deal ($550B investment, 15% tariff)
**Truth Social post**: *"We just completed a massive Deal with Japan, perhaps the largest Deal ever made."*
**Market reaction**: S&P +0.78% — **new high.** NASDAQ +0.61%. Japanese auto ADRs (**TM +13.9%, HMC +11%**) — a single announcement drove a double-digit one-day gain in Japanese auto names.

### July 27, 2025 — EU deal (15% tariff)
**Truth Social post**: Following the Trump/von der Leyen Scotland announcement: *"biggest Trade Deal ever."*
**Market reaction**: Monday July 28 S&P +0.02%, NASDAQ +0.33% — already priced in.

The Deal is that South Korea will give to the United States $350 Billion Dollars for Investments owned and controlled by the United States, and selected by myself, as President."*

**Truth Social**: After meeting Xi at APEC Busan, Trump posted *"We have a deal."* Agreement: 10% tariff cut if China cooperates on fentanyl, normalization of rare-earth exports for 1 year.
**Market reaction (Mon 11/3)**: S&P +0.7%, SOX +1.4% (NVDA +1.2%, AMD +2.1%).

### ★ December 8, 2025 (Monday) — NVIDIA H200 China export approved ★
**Truth Social post**: *"I am pleased to announce that we have agreed to allow NVIDIA to sell their H200 product to approved customers in China. President Xi has responded positively. The United States will receive 25% of all sales... This policy will support American Jobs, strengthen U.S. Manufacturing, and benefit American Taxpayers."*
